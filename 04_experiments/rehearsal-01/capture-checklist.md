# Capture Checklist

## Before you go
- [ ] Phone charged, **storage free** (slow-mo files are big)
- [ ] Tripod or a stable spot (backpack, fence, a ledge) + phone clamp
- [ ] Painter's tape and a tape measure
- [ ] A partner for clips 03–05 (or plan them for another day)
- [ ] Note your phone model: ____________________

## Camera settings
- [ ] Highest steady frame rate your phone offers (120 or 240 slow-mo is ideal; 60 is OK)
- [ ] Write the setting you chose: ______ fps at ______ resolution
- [ ] Lock focus/exposure if your camera allows it (long-press on iPhone; "Pro" mode on Android)
- [ ] Turn off any "auto frame rate" / "action mode" / stabilization if the phone lets you

## Clock test (do this first, 1 minute)
- [ ] Open a stopwatch with milliseconds on a second phone or a laptop
- [ ] Film it for ~5 seconds with your chosen settings
- [ ] Later: check that the video's timestamps advance at the same pace as the stopwatch (a screen only refreshes 60–120 times a second, so this is a rough check, not a lab test)

## Placement
- [ ] **Side-on** to the shooter's right or left, about hip height, far enough to see the **whole body + ball + defender's hand** for the full rep
- [ ] Same spot for all 5 clips. **Tape an X where the tripod stands**
- [ ] Tape a line on the floor where the shooter catches, and another where the defender starts (clips 04–05)
- [ ] Bright, even light; avoid shooting straight into a window

## Recording
- [ ] Start recording **before** the pass, stop **after** landing
- [ ] Say the clip number out loud at the start ("clip zero-four")
- [ ] One rep per clip (keeps the files simple)
- [ ] Note anything odd right after each clip in the ledger notes (fatigue 1–5, a bad pass, someone walked through)

## Privacy
- [ ] Only you and your partner in frame, and the partner agrees to be filmed
- [ ] Don't post these clips anywhere
