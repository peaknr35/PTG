Film room · concept sheet · v1.0

Does your practicesurvive a defender?

Practice-to-Game Lab is a reasoning mentor for players who want to get better but don't know what to work on. It learns from your own mistakes, teaches the reasoning behind the fix, gives you a way to practice it, checks whether it shows up in your next game, and only calls advice proven for you after it holds on a retest.

Phase 0 · measuring by hand Learning lab + portfolio No code yet, on purpose
00:01 · The gap

You can work hard and still not know what to fix

Most players who want to improve put in the hours: drills, film, pickup. Without someone who can look at their mistakes and say "work on this, and here's why," the same mistakes keep showing up in games. The game asks for three different things, and practice without guidance mostly builds one.

00:02 · The system

The loop that never closes

Seen as a system, solo training has a loop that runs fine and a loop that's broken. Every product in this space speeds up the first one. PTG exists to close the second.

Runs on its own

Practice loop

practice → gym skill → confidence → more practice

Feels like progress. Workout apps tune this loop: more reps, better plans.

Broken today

Game loop

game mistake → noticed → practice changed → better game

Game results almost never come back into practice. A mistake on Saturday gets fixed weeks later, or never.

00:03 · The chain

Seven steps, one loop

Every lesson runs the whole chain. Skip a step and it stops being PTG.

  1. 01
    Pause before the decisionA clip freezes just before the read. You commit to an answer first.
  2. 02
    Name the cueThe reveal shows what to look at and at least one other good option, from a coach's notes.
  3. 03
    Beginner versionThe pro's read, cut down to the version you can run at your level.
  4. 04
    Your own filmThe same read in your clips, makes next to misses, with honest error bars.
  5. 05
    Floor repOne solo rep that fits your space, time and partner, with a cue that fades.
  6. 06
    Game assignment + tagWhat to look for next game, logged in ten seconds: saw it, acted, outcome.
  7. 07
    RetestA delayed, cue-free check. Only then does the lesson become "proven for you."

00:05 · Honesty is the feature

Every card shows its grade

AI coaches sound sure of everything. PTG labels how well each piece of advice is supported, and it can say "can't tell."

AStrong researchMeta-analyses or trials, tested for transfer.
BEarly researchSmall studies, one sport, or youth only.
CCoach's callCoaching practice or a hypothesis to test.
PProven for youHeld on your own delayed retest.

Who checks the advice

Right now there's no coach in the loop, so every card goes through two checks before a player sees it:

  1. 1
    Automatic claim auditEvery number traces to a measurement, every claim carries its grade, and nothing PTG never claims gets through.
  2. 2
    Founder reviewThe founder, an experienced player but not a certified coach, reads it. Reviewed advice is labeled C · coach's call until your own retest makes it P · proven for you.

Only cards that change what you do next get reviewed. When review time outgrows the founder's weekly limit, or the pilot passes five players, this step moves to a coach partner or a coach-review tier.

Why the empty gym lies

In an empty gym nobody closes out, so a slow release never gets punished. Build a scenario, compare the empty gym with live defense, and get one experiment to try. It's a "what if" model: it shows time and space, never why you missed.

    00:07 · Requirements

    What the product must do

    The seven business requirements from PRD v1.0. Each one has functional requirements and a test behind it.

    BR-1See whether a contest reached the ball before release, on your own clips
    BR-2Study a play that pauses before the decision; answer, then see the cue
    BR-3Get a beginner version of the read and a floor rep that fits your space
    BR-4End every lesson with a game assignment and a 10-second game tag
    BR-5"Proven for you" only after a delayed, cue-free retest
    BR-6Every piece of advice shows its grade and what it's based on
    BR-7Your video and data are private by default and fully deletable
